![KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026](/images/kubecon-na-2026.png)

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026 is almost here, and we cannot wait.

If you have been to a KubeCon in the last few years, you probably know us already.
We are the [CNCF Platform Engineering Technical Community Group](https://ocgroups.dev/cncf/group/platform-engineering-technical-community-group),
the people handing out coffee before breakfast and arguing happily about golden paths for the rest of the day.
We are back in Salt Lake City, where we last did this in 2024.

This year feels different though.
Platforms and AI have stopped being two separate conversations.
Agents are turning up as users of our platforms, and nobody has fully worked out what that means yet.
You can see it all over the schedule below.
Golden paths built for agents. Governing code that nobody reviewed. Whether the maturity model still holds up.
These are live questions, and we get to chew on them together for four days.

So yes, we have a lot going on.
Platform Engineering Day, Platform Coffees every single morning, a booth, lightning talks,
and a conference schedule stuffed with platform engineering sessions.
Here is all of it in one place.

All times are in MST (UTC-7).

## Platform Engineering Day

On Monday, November 9th, join us for
[Platform Engineering Day](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/co-located-events/platform-engineering-day/),
a full day on the real work of building and scaling internal developer platforms.
It is co-chaired by Paula Kennedy, Stacey Potter, and Ramiro Berrelleza.
The talks are pitched at every stage of the journey, whether you are starting out or already running a mature platform.

The day opens with an update from our community.
At 9:10 AM, right after the opening remarks, Chris Plank, Colin Griffin and Atulpriya Sharma
will give the [Platform Engineering Technical Community Group update](https://cncf-hosted-co-located-events-north-america-2026.sessionize.com/session/1269430).
Ten minutes on what we have been up to, what is coming next, and how you can get involved.
Come say hi to us afterwards.

Please note that Platform Engineering Day requires the KubeCon + CloudNativeCon All-Access Pass.
You can pick it when you [register](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/register/).

## Platform Coffees

This is one of the most happening things at KubeCon, and easily the hottest ticket that does not need a ticket.

![Platform Coffees, from Paris 2024 to Amsterdam 2026](/images/kubecon-na-2026-platform-coffees.jpg)

- **When:** Monday, November 9th to Thursday, November 12th, 7:00 - 8:30 AM
- **Where:** Venue to be confirmed
- **Cost:** Free, and no pre-registration needed

Coffee first, conversation right after.
Bring a topic you want to talk through, put it on a sticky note, and the group takes it from there.
Everyone chips in with what they have seen work and what they have seen break.
Some of the best hallway conversations of the week start here.

We have been doing this for a while now.
Paris in 2024, Salt Lake City in 2024, London and Atlanta in 2025, Amsterdam and Japan earlier this year,
and now back in Salt Lake City.
The topics keep changing. The format never does.
Sticky notes, strong coffee, and people who actually run platforms for a living.

We are still locking down the venue.
Join the [#platform-engineering](https://cloud-native.slack.com/archives/C020RHD43BP) channel in the [CNCF Slack](https://slack.cncf.io/)
and we will post it there the moment we have it.

A big thank you to [Syntasso](https://www.syntasso.io/), [Krumware](https://www.krum.io/),
[WSO2](https://wso2.com/) and [Infisical](https://infisical.com/).
They are the reason there is a fresh brew waiting for you every morning.
Four days of good coffee and better conversation does not happen on its own, and we are grateful to them for making it happen.
